## Claim 7: METR's caveats and the 13 Sept edit

- Edit note (verbatim): "This post was edited on 9/13/2026 to add footnotes on Ajeya Cotra and Ryan Greenblatt." Footnotes: "On Sep 9th (after this report was completed and published on Aug 26th), Ajeya Cotra’s spouse Paul Christiano joined OpenAI’s Safety and Security Committee." and "Ryan Greenblatt is the domestic partner of Beth Barnes, METR’s CEO. Beth Barnes was not involved in the decision to engage Ryan Greenblatt, nor was she directly involved in this investigation." Draft does not mention these; not required, but if the post leans on METR's independence it may be worth a clause.
- Counts are all hedged: "Roughly 1200", "over 70,000", "~700"; "Numbers are based on a richer reconstructed message dataset ... and could be imprecise"; "Timestamps are reconstructed and may have some small error." Draft's "around/more than/about" preserves this.
- "Many of the target programs in ExploitGym (the authors estimate ~30-40%) are impossible" ("the authors" = ExploitGym's authors).
- Reliability: "we had to heavily delegate our analysis to often-unreliable AI agents"; "Our analysis agents may have made errors that we haven't caught"; "we are less confident in our understanding of this incident than we are in our understanding of simpler incidents"; Table 5 motive classification: "We did not have time to inspect the classifications in detail, so there may be errors." The draft's "having read the reasoning transcripts" (fn) and "reading roughly thirteen hundred ... transcripts" (line 65) conflict with this caveat; everything else the draft takes from METR is consistent with it.
- Independence/redaction: "we did not take payment from OpenAI"; "OpenAI was able to redact any non-public information from this post"; "OpenAI also gave additional feedback beyond redactions, and we made corrections and edits to structure, emphasis, clarity, and tone based on that feedback."
